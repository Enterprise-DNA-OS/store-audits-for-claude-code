# Retail checks and evidence handling

Checked 1 November 2026. This is an operational review aid. It does not certify a store, decide whether the law has been breached, or replace a retailer's review of applicable rules. It contains no food-safety, payroll, payment or regulated product-release system.

| Check | Basis | What the program does |
|---|---|---|
| PRICE_DISPLAY_REVIEW | [NZ Commerce Commission pricing guidance](https://www.comcom.govt.nz/consumers/dealing-with-typical-situations/buying-goods-and-services/pricing/) and [ACCC price displays](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/pricing/price-displays) | Flags a recorded failed PRICE check in an NZ or AU store until an action is verified. The seeded checklist compares shelf and checkout prices. A failed answer is an operator observation, not a legal determination. |
| EVIDENCE_REVIEW | [NZ Privacy Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) and [OAIC APP 11](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/australian-privacy-principles/australian-privacy-principles-guidelines/chapter-11-app-11-security-of-personal-information) | Flags an evidence reference after its operator-chosen review date. Requires a purpose and access owner. No statutory retention interval is inferred. Review legal holds, permitted purposes and whether Australian privacy law applies to your organisation. |
| FAILURE_WITHOUT_ACTION | Internal operating policy | Lists failed answers with no action. Local audits cannot be submitted with such a gap. |
| OVERDUE_ACTION | Internal operating policy | Lists work past its chosen due date until independently verified. |
| UNANSWERED_IMPORT | Import assurance | Lists historic answers whose exact labels have not been mapped. Imported history is never counted as a locally validated score. |

An evidence reference does not prove that the file exists or that access is restricted. Inspect the underlying material. Keep photos containing people to the minimum necessary and review purpose and access before sharing. The command never deletes records or sends information. Erasure and de-identification require a separately reviewed process covering the database, source exports, history and backups. Do not put unnecessary personal information into free text or the source import.

Submitted and imported response records are immutable. Use a new visit for changed observations. Evidence reviews and action changes have before/after history. Actor labels support accountability within a trusted team but are not authenticated identities. A shared installation needs authenticated roles, restricted database access and tested backup recovery.
